How to play

  • Aim: hover / move your finger over the table. Use ◀ ▶ for fine aim.
  • Aim sway — mouse controlled: moving your mouse (or finger) left and right pushes the sway left and right — the dot under the table follows your mouse. Nudge it to line up the guide, drag the sway meter directly, or scroll the mouse wheel over the table for fine control. Drag the Sway amount slider to make the sway wider or narrower, tap Center sway to reset it to the middle, tap Mouse controls sway to switch to the old automatic swing, or tap Sway on/off to turn it off.
  • Shoot: drag back on the table and release, or set Power and tap Shoot. Try Break shot to open a new rack.
  • Bounce: the cushions are springy — balls bounce lively off the rails and off each other, roll long, and scatter wide on the break. Use the banks!
  • Levels: 30 levels in 3 stages — Silver (Levels 1–10), Gold (11–20) and Platinum (21–30). Clear a table to unlock the next level; finishing Level 10 or 20 moves you up a stage. Tap any unlocked level in the level panel to replay it.
  • Skip to next stage: for 1000 coins ($10) at $5 = 500 coins you can skip from Silver to Gold or Gold to Platinum, even without finishing the current stage. You do not get the stage-completion bonus for the stage you skip, and there is no skip on Platinum — it is the last stage.
  • Goal: pot all 15 coloured balls in any order before your shots run out. The shot limit drops from 30 on Level 1 to 18 on Level 30.
  • Getting harder: every level adds a little more aim sway and slightly tighter pockets, on top of the shrinking shot limit. Better cues (below) help counter the sway.
  • Scratch: if the white cue ball drops in a pocket, it’s spotted back on the table and you lose 5 coins (unless you have a Scratch Shield).
  • Coins: +10 per ball potted, a clear bonus that grows every level (27 🪙 on Level 1 up to 68 🪙 on Level 30), plus stage bonuses: +100 🪙 for finishing Silver, +150 🪙 for Gold, +250 🪙 for Platinum. Spend them in the Coin shop.
  • Coin packs: in the Coin shop, from $5 = 500 coins up to $30 = 3,000 coins ($10 = 1,000, $15 = 1,500, $20 = 2,000, $25 = 2,500) at $1 = 100 coins. Tap a pack to add its coins to your balance — demo / in-game purchase only, no real money is charged yet.
  • Cue upgrades: spend coins on better cues — Steady (less sway), Power (harder shots), Precision (longer guide + prediction), and the Golden Cue (best of all). Unlocking one pays a coin bonus and earns a badge; tap Equip on any cue you own to use it.
